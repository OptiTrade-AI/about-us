## Scan the Entire Market

Three quantitative pipelines run the numbers so you don't have to.

**CSP Screener** scores cash-secured put opportunities from 0 to 100 using Bollinger Band positioning, annualized return, delta risk, liquidity, IV quality, and break-even cushion. Not a heatmap of raw data — a ranked list of the best income trades available right now.

**Aggressive Options Scanner** catches RSI extremes confirmed by MACD and Bollinger Bands. When a stock is statistically oversold or overbought with technical confirmation, it surfaces the setup and tracks changes day over day.

**Options Flow Analyzer** runs 11 anomaly detection algorithms across 3,800+ tickers — volume explosions, IV spikes, delta clustering, put/call ratio skew, gamma exposure, contract concentration. When smart money is moving, this finds it.

Run any pipeline on demand. Watch progress in real time. Filter results with presets. Create trades directly from the results.

## AI That Knows Your Book

This is where OptiTrade is fundamentally different. Every AI agent has real-time access to your actual portfolio — positions, cost basis, premium history, Greeks, capital allocation. It's not giving you generic advice. It's advising *you*, about *your trades*, with *live data*.

### Before You Trade

**Trade Check** validates every new trade before you enter it — risk, Greeks, portfolio fit, concentration warnings. It's built into every add-trade modal.

**CSP Optimizer** takes the pipeline's scored opportunities and goes deeper. Select your top candidates, and an AI agent pulls the live options chain, checks your existing exposure, searches for catalysts, and delivers three strategy lanes with position sizing and assignment scenarios.

**CC Optimizer** does the same for covered calls — six strategy modes depending on whether you're underwater or above water, with target returns, catalyst research, and recovery projections.

### While You're In a Trade

**Exit Coach** gives you a clear HOLD, CLOSE, or ROLL verdict on any open position, with full reasoning.

**Roll Advisor** pulls the best roll candidates from the live chain when it's time to move.

**Smart Alerts** continuously flags positions that need attention — expiring soon, too much heat, Greeks out of balance.

**Earnings Watch** warns you when open positions have upcoming earnings or catalysts.

### Understanding the Market

**Daily Movers** investigates why your holdings moved today — catalysts, news sentiment, intraday charts, sector context, and how it impacts your positions.

**Flow Analyzer** digs into unusual options activity flagged by the screener — classifies the signal, assesses risk/reward, and tells you whether it aligns with your existing book.

**AI Trading Coach** is a full conversational chat that knows your entire portfolio. Ask it anything.

### After the Trade

**Behavioral Patterns** reviews your history to detect biases — revenge trading, closing winners too early, position sizing mistakes, strategy drift.

**Trading Journal** pairs daily entries and mood tracking with AI-powered weekly reflections that correlate your mindset with your actual results.

### Full Transparency

Every AI agent records a complete trace — every tool call, every reasoning step, every result. Replay any analysis to see exactly how a recommendation was made. No black boxes.

## How It Works

**Market data** flows from Polygon.io — institutional-grade stock prices, options chains, Greeks, intraday candles, earnings dates. Prices refresh every 60 seconds during market hours.

**Pipelines** scan the full ticker universe using technical indicators and options analysis, score every opportunity, and deliver results to your dashboard with live progress updates.

**AI agents** layer intelligence on top. They call live market data, search the web for catalysts and news, read your portfolio and trade history, and synthesize everything into clear, actionable recommendations — streaming results as they work.

**One-click trade creation** connects screeners and AI agents directly to your journal. "Write This Put" or "Write This Call" creates the trade with every field pre-filled.
